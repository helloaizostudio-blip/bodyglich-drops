# Ep 8 — "I Ate The Worst Cursed Fruit 🫧" — Voice Script

About 60 seconds. **Bold** = punch the word. *(…)* = delivery note. `/` = tiny pause.

| Character | Voice | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **Mako** (narrator, captain) | Blaze voice (am_michael), slightly younger | Cocky, fast, always half-smiling, even when things go wrong |
| **Pip** (navigator) | af_bella | Flat, dry, unimpressed. Never raises her voice |
| **Captain Gorr** (villain) | am_onyx, pitched down | Slow, heavy, amused. He thinks this is a joke |

---

**1. MAKO** *(urgent, hooky, straight in)*
In my world, eat a cursed fruit and you get a **superpower**. / But touch the sea, and you sink **forever**.

**2. MAKO** *(storyteller, proud of the world)*
Every pirate has a bounty. The scariest one is worth a **billion** coins.

**3. MAKO** *(beat, deadpan)*
Mine is three **coins**.

**4. PIP** *(flat, not even looking up)*
Honestly? / That's **generous**.

**5. MAKO** *(trying to sound cool, failing)*
Our ship is a rowboat. / With a **leak**.

**6. MAKO** *(listing, quick, proud then awkward)*
My crew? A cook who burns **water**. / And Crate. Super strong. / Wears a **box**.

**7. MAKO** *(excited, speeding up)*
Then I found a cursed fruit. I ate the **whole** thing.

**8. MAKO** *(big build-up… then small)*
My power? / I can blow **bubbles**.

**9. MAKO** *(sulky)*
Pirates laughed so hard one fell off his **ship**.

**10. MAKO** *(defensive, the plant: say "float" clearly)*
They don't pop. They just float. / **Really** float.

**11. PIP** *(flat)*
So it's **useless**.

**12. MAKO** *(tone drops, serious, slower)*
Then the billion-coin captain found **us**.

**13. GORR** *(low, slow, amused)*
Cute… **boat**.

**14. MAKO** *(fast, shocked)*
One punch. / No more **boat**.

**15. MAKO** *(panic, quick)*
Pip can't swim. / And I can't touch the **sea**.

**16. MAKO** *(whisper, the idea lands)*
But bubbles… **float**.

**17. MAKO** *(rising, heroic, gets louder each sentence)*
I blew the biggest bubble of my life. / Pip floated up. / Then I bubbled **myself**.

**18. GORR** *(laughing, booming)*
Bubbles can't stop **me**!

**19. MAKO** *(cocky grin is back)*
He was right. / But they can **lift** you.

**20. MAKO** *(calm, satisfied, slow)*
Last I saw, he was floating somewhere above the **clouds**.

**21. MAKO** *(can't hide the excitement)*
Next morning, my bounty was thirty **million**.

**22. PIP** *(dry, but a tiny smile)*
Still the worst fruit?

**23. MAKO** *(beaming, short)*
Best **fruit**.

---
Every line is written in my own words. No quotes or catchphrases from any anime.
