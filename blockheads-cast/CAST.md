# Blockheads — Cast (v2)

Character Studio: https://claude.ai/artifact/Q7gBgDs7harYf1HFRNf4MZ (the v1 viewer stays at https://claude.ai/artifact/P1NXWJgTW4Ki2kT7sEjZau)

Every character is an original block design built in code (three.js) from a JSON spec. See README.md for the spec fields. Expressions: default, happy, sad, shocked, angry, smug. Motions: idle, walk, talk, wave.

**Renamed:** the narrator Leo is now **Zash**. Their id is still `leo`, so existing episodes keep working.

## Main
- **Zash** (formerly Leo): The Narrator. The "I" in every story. An ordinary kid who keeps ending up with strange rules and powers. Sarcastic, a little lazy, secretly kind. Has a crush on Somi he thinks nobody knows about (everyone knows). _Look: messy hair · hoodie._ id `leo`
- **Mia**: The Rich Girl. The richest kid at school whom nobody talks to. Quiet, a bit guarded, with a secret mirror routine. Her ponytail bow costs more than Zash's bike. _Look: ponytail hair · open · earrings, bow, necklace._ id `mia`
- **Old Ezra**: Wish Store Owner. Keeps the receipts for every wish ever sold. Never looks up from the counter. _Look: sides hair · waistcoat._ id `ezra`
- **Nova**: The New Girl. Transferred mid-year. Calm, sharp and one second ahead of everyone. _Look: bob hair · leather · earrings._ id `nova`
- **Somi** `NEW`: The Girl Zash Likes. Artsy, funny and impossible to impress. Always has paint on her sleeve and a sketchbook full of drawings she won't show anyone. Zash has rehearsed saying hi to her 400 times. _Look: wavy hair · cardigan · hoops, bow._ id `somi`
- **Tobi** `NEW`: Zash's Best Friend. Zash's best friend and worst advisor. Confident about everything, right about nothing. Makes beats on his phone and narrates his own life like a trailer. _Look: afro hair · bomber · headphones._ id `tobi`

## Family
- **Mom**: Zash's Mom. Warm and observant. She can tell when "I'm fine" isn't true, even without a counter. _Look: long hair · cardigan · earrings._ id `mom`
- **Dad**: Zash's Dad. Big beard, bigger lie counter (3,000 and counting). His "five minutes" turned out to be a to-do list, not a lie. _Look: swept hair · wristwatch._ id `dad`
- **Max**: Little Brother. Six years old, 212 lies. Most of them about who ate the cookies. _Look: spiky hair._ id `max`
- **Nana June**: Grandma. Remembers everything, which is rare in a world where memories buy wishes. _Look: bun hair · cardigan · earrings, pearls._ id `nana`
- **Liza** `NEW`: Zash's Cousin. Zash's chaotic younger cousin who visits every holiday and leaves with all his secrets. Gamer, prankster, master negotiator. Will absolutely tell Somi. _Look: spacebuns hair · overalls · headset._ id `liza`

## School
- **Ms. Grimm**: Strict Teacher. Has not smiled since 2009. Gives surprise tests that Zash is never surprised by. _Look: bun hair · earrings._ id `grimm`
- **Benny**: The Nerd. Overexcited, with glasses and a hoodie. Always has a theory about what is really going on. _Look: spiky hair · ziphoodie · backpack._ id `benny`
- **Theo**: The Bookworm. Always carrying a stack of books and always polite. His lie counter might be the second lowest in town. _Look: swept hair · books._ id `theo`
- **Gus**: School Janitor. Knows every secret in the building. The first person Zash compliments. _Look: buzz hair · beanie · overalls · keys._ id `gus`
- **Brick**: The Bully. Wears his cap backwards and smirks a lot. Broke, because he only ever says mean things. _Look: buzz hair · backcap · wristband._ id `brick`
- **Pixel** `NEW`: Young Hacker. Fourteen, fluent in six programming languages and zero social ones. Hacked the school bell once to buy everyone 4 more minutes of lunch. Teal undercut, neon headset, always typing. _Look: undercut hair · hoodie · headset, laptop._ id `pixel`

## Town
- **Dr. Rao**: Doctor. Overworked, kind and always in a mask. Good for any hospital or lab scene. _Look: messy hair · labcoat · lanyard._ id `rao`
- **Shade**: The Detective. Fedora, dark glasses, all black. He shows up whenever a story needs a mystery. _Look: slick hair · fedora · trench · gloves._ id `detective`
- **Mr. Goldbank**: The Tycoon. The richest man in town. Has never paid anyone a compliment. Use him for anything about money. _Look: swept hair · suit · fur, briefcase, watch._ id `goldbank`
- **Captain Reyes**: Ship Captain. Cheerful captain with a ring of keys to every door on the ship. Good for travel or sea stories. _Look: long hair · captain · coat2 · fur, keys, earrings._ id `reyes`
- **Agent K**: Man in a Suit. Black suit and a thin tie. Nobody knows who he works for. Use him for a "they're watching" twist. _Look: slick hair · suit · earpiece._ id `agent`
- **Dave**: Tired Office Guy. Has been "almost done" with one spreadsheet for three years. Glasses, stubble and a plaster on his cheek. _Look: spiky hair · suit · mug, lanyard._ id `dave`
- **Rex**: Tough Guy. Leather jacket, goatee and a permanent squint. Looks scary but cries at movies. _Look: messy hair · leather._ id `rex`
- **Silas Vale** `NEW`: Mysterious Businessman. Owns half the town and no one has seen him sign anything. Shows up exactly when a deal is too good to be true. Always smiling, never blinking. _Look: slick hair · coat2 · gloves, ring, pocketsquare._ id `vale`
- **Officer Brooks** `NEW`: Police Officer. By the book, except for the book of jokes she keeps in her pocket. Has written exactly one ticket in her career, to herself. _Look: bun hair · police · radio, earrings._ id `brooks`
- **Dex** `NEW`: Street-Smart Teen. Knows every shortcut, every vendor and every rumour in town. Sells "slightly used" phone cases. Trustworthy about 60% of the time. _Look: buzz hair · bucket · track · necklace, phone._ id `dex`
- **Valentina Sterling** `NEW`: Wealthy Socialite. Has never touched a door handle. Throws parties for her dog's birthday. Mr. Goldbank's biggest rival, and she wins. _Look: wavy hair · tweed · pearls, headshades, handbag._ id `val`
- **Dr. Ada Quill** `NEW`: Scientist. Runs the lab on the hill where all the weird rules seem to come from. Has blown up her eyebrows twice. Says "fascinating" right before something goes wrong. _Look: frizzy hair · labcoat · goggles, gloves, clipboard._ id `quill`
- **Moe Okafor** `NEW`: Mechanic. Can fix anything with a wrench and a stern look. Talks to engines more kindly than to customers. Secret talent: opera. _Look: buzz hair · bandana · wrench, gloves._ id `moe`
- **Penny Park** `NEW`: Journalist. Reporter for the Blocktown Bugle. Asks the question everyone is thinking, loudly, in public. Her notebook is 90% conspiracy theories, 10% grocery lists. _Look: bob hair · beret · trench · notepad, camera, lanyard._ id `penny`
- **Chef Marco** `NEW`: Restaurant Owner. Owns Marco's, the only restaurant in town. Every dish is "his grandmother's recipe," including the cereal. Will cry if you ask for ketchup. _Look: curly hair · chef · chef · bandana, apron, spoon._ id `marco`
- **Jet Okoye** `NEW`: Pro Athlete. Town basketball legend. Fastest person in Blocktown, slowest texter alive. Trains at 5am and makes everyone else feel bad about it. _Look: highpony hair · headband · wristband, ball, medal._ id `jet`
- **Sgt. Hank Mercer** `NEW`: Retired Soldier. Retired after 30 years and now commands the community garden like a battlefield. Gruff, loyal, secretly the softest heart in town. Salutes Biscuit the dog. _Look: flattop hair · beret · fieldjacket · dogtags, medals, cane._ id `mercer`

## Extras
- **Kip**: Lost Survivor. Torn clothes and no shoes. Has been stuck somewhere for a very long time. Good for a "paused for years" ending. _Look: spiky hair._ id `kip`
- **Sora**: The Traveler. Hair over the eyes, a scarf and a duffel bag. Always arriving or leaving. Good for a mysterious stranger. _Look: emo hair · scarf, duffel._ id `sora`
- **Wren** `NEW`: Mysterious Traveller. Nobody knows where Wren comes from, only that the lantern never goes out. Speaks in riddles and gives very specific directions to places that don't exist yet. _Look: long hair · hood · cloak · lantern, map, backpack._ id `wren`
