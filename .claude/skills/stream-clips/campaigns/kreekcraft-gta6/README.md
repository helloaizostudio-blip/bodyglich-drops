# KreekCraft GTA 6 clipping campaign (ClipLaunch / Content Rewards)

Run:

    streamclips clip --find --topic "GTA 6" --keywords "gta 6,gta vi,gta6" --campaign kreekcraft-gta6 --when week --count 3
    streamclips clip <KreekCraft stream URL> --topic "GTA 6" --campaign kreekcraft-gta6 --section "1:00:00-3:00:00"

What the preset does, from the campaign brief:
- burns the official @KreekCraft logo overlay (kreekcraft-logo.png) into every clip, start to finish
- every POSTING.md caption ends with "Watch KreekCraft on YouTube and Twitch!" and has #GTA6 #KreekCraft #GrandTheftAutoVI (no links)
- tells the picker to favour KreekCraft's reactions, chases, fails and discoveries, with accurate titles

Rules the tool can't enforce. You have to do these yourself:
- Source: only KreekCraft's own GTA 6 streams and replays (YouTube @KreekCraft or Twitch). No other creators, and no reposting other clippers' edits.
- Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts only. Keep likes visible. Keep each clip public for at least 30 days.
- Audience: at least 40% Tier 1 (US, UK, CA, AU). Follow the campaign's Warm Up Guide on new accounts and read the Approval Process.
- Engagement: at least 0.8%. No story boosting, bought views or engagement manipulation.
- Don't post the same or a nearly identical clip more than 5 times.
- Pay: $1 CPM, up to $300 per clip. Submit clips at contentrewards.com.

**Update 2026-11-22: the KreekCraft campaign has ended.** The channel continues as a fan-clip channel: no logo overlay, no paid-promotion label, credit KreekCraft in every caption.
