**Tagline:** *my family is a little misprinted*

## 5. Posting plan for the first 2 weeks

- **1 episode a day**, the same video on both platforms, around **7–9 PM IST**.
- **Keep the cast and the look identical every time.** Viewers follow a series they recognise.
- **No "follow me" lines in videos.** End on the punchline, not a request.
- **After 7 posts, check which hook kept people watching**, then make more like it.
  - The measure is how long people watch (Instagram and YouTube Shorts analytics), not likes.
- **Topic bank** (same cast, same format):
  1. Grandma replies "you too" to everything, including the waiter, the pilot and the priest.
  2. Grandpa waves back at someone who was waving at the person behind him, and has kept waving since 1979.
  3. Nobody in the family will take the last slice of cake. It has been on the table since 2003.
  4. The grandson said "see you later" and they kept walking the same way. He moved house to escape.
  5. Grandma's "quick trip to the shop" is now in its fourth year.

## 6. AI disclosure
